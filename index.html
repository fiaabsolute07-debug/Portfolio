Saint Gard

Marketing intern · Apr — Jun 2026

Objective

Saint Gard sells premium menswear (VND 1.5–6.5M) through Facebook ads that open a Messenger chat. My job: get more customer messages, for less, from a daily budget of around VND 1.5M at peak, by finding the images and offers that work.

My scope

  • Wrote every prompt and made 90+ static ads alone with AI: Claude for prompts, Google Flow for images
  • Studied competitor ads in Meta Ad Library and on fanpages and proposed the direction: product shots with an offer
  • Proposed 40+ image and offer tests; once the lead approved, each ran about a day and the ad with more messages at a lower cost stayed on
  • Wrote the daily and weekly reports the lead used in weekly budget reviews; made ~60 product visuals for the web store

Result (ad data 11 May – 5 Jun 2026)

  • −24%cost per message at the same ~VND 1M daily spend: 31.9K (13 May) to 24.3K (4 Jun); ~22–25K after the first week
  • 451customer messages in 26 days, VND 29.3K each on average
  • 90+static creatives in 2 months
