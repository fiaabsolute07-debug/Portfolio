07
how i work

the same loop, every time — try fast, learn faster

I map before I move.
When I take on a task, I break it into steps and get clear on what the goal actually is — before I gather tools or resources. Clarity first, motion second.

I work well within a team.
I'm comfortable taking direction, coordinating with others, and owning my part of a shared goal. I like the autonomy to figure out how, as long as I'm clear on the what and the why.

I orchestrate; AI executes.
I split the work into pieces, hand each piece to the right AI tool, then review, refine, and decide what ships. AI does the heavy lifting — the judgment stays mine.

I move fast and learn faster.
I'd rather try, get feedback, and fix quickly than wait for perfect. I take feedback as fuel, not criticism — the faster the loop, the faster I improve.

I'm not afraid of being wrong. I'm afraid of being stuck.
A mistake I learn from is progress. The only failure I avoid is repeating the same one twice.

I keep people in the loop.
I'd rather over-communicate than leave a manager guessing. Clear updates, no surprises.
